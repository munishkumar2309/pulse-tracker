Pulse privacy policy

Pulse is a personal app. It has no server and no account. Everything it records is stored only in the app's storage on this phone.

Health data

With your permission, Pulse reads steps, sleep, weight and resting heart rate from Health Connect to show your progress and a readiness estimate. It does not write to Health Connect and never sends health data anywhere.

SMS

With your permission, Pulse reads SMS from bank and card senders to record transactions. Messages are read on the phone; nothing is uploaded. One-time passwords and personal messages are ignored.

Internet use

Pulse uses the internet only when you search packaged foods or scan a barcode. It then sends the search words or barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) and nothing else.

Backups and deletion

Android cloud backup is turned off for Pulse. You can download a backup file in Settings and delete all data at any time.
